# MIEN — Growth Feature Plan

*How to attract users, make them stay, and turn them into advocates — stage by stage*

---

## Growth Framework: AARRR for Each Stage

Every feature is tagged by its growth role:

| Tag | Role | Question It Answers |
|---|---|---|
| **ACQUIRE** | Get new users to download | "Why should I try this app?" |
| **ACTIVATE** | First-time user → first "wow" moment | "This app actually works for me" |
| **RETAIN** | Keep users coming back daily/weekly | "I need to check this every morning" |
| **REVENUE** | Convert free → paying | "This is worth paying for" |
| **REFER** | Turn users into recruiters | "You NEED to try this app" |

## Stage 0: Foundation (Now → Week 4)

*Growth goal: Build the minimum that can deliver ONE magical moment*

### The "Wow" Moment We're Designing For

> User adds 5 items from their closet → instantly sees a styled outfit that matches their body, style, and today's weather → thinks "holy shit, this actually works"

This moment is what every competitor fails to deliver. It is the ONLY thing that matters at Stage 0.

---

### ACTIVATE: The 5-Minute Magic

| Feature | What It Does | Why It Drives Growth |
|---|---|---|
| **"Quick Start" wardrobe** — add just 3-5 items to get your first outfit | Instead of requiring full wardrobe upload, generate value from minimal input. Show outfit suggestion after 3 items. | Every competitor loses 60-80% of users at catalog step. We skip it entirely. Users who see value in 5 minutes will add more items voluntarily over days. |
| **AI snap-to-catalog** — take photo → auto-detect category, color, material | One tap: photo → cropped + background removed + tagged. User just confirms. | Reduces per-item time from 2 minutes (Stylebook) to 10 seconds. This alone is a competitive moat. |
| **"Your first outfit" celebration** — after 3 items, show animated outfit card with match score | Full-screen reveal: "87% match for your style + body." Feels like unwrapping a gift. | Emotional payoff at minute 3-5, not hour 8-15. This is where the hook sets. |

### RETAIN: The Daily Reason to Open

| Feature | What It Does | Why It Drives Growth |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning outfit notification** — push at user's wake-up time: "Today's outfit is ready" | One tap → full-screen outfit styled for today's weather, their body, their mood. | Creates a daily habit. The app becomes part of the morning routine like checking weather. Cladwell tried this but broke it with bad suggestions. We have better data (body + style + weather). |
| **Weather-aware feed** — outfit suggestions change with real forecast | Today is 28°C and humid → linen, light colors, no layers. Tomorrow drops to 15°C → sweater + jacket combos surface. | Relevance = retention. Generic suggestions = churn. When users see the feed actually respond to weather, they trust the app. |

## Stage 1: Closed Beta (Week 5-10)

*Growth goal: 50-100 beta users who can't stop talking about the app*

### ACTIVATE: Remove Every Remaining Friction

| Feature | What It Does | Why It Drives Growth |
|---|---|---|
| **Bulk photo upload** (10-50 items) | Select photos from camera roll in batch → AI processes all simultaneously → user reviews/confirms tags in a swipe-through flow | The #1 complaint in the category, solved. Users who tried Stylebook/Whering and quit will try us specifically because of this. Reddit/TikTok will amplify "finally an app that doesn't make you spend 8 hours uploading." |
| **Smart defaults from style quiz** | Use onboarding style quiz answers to pre-weight outfit suggestions before any items are added. Show "outfits in your style" using a starter catalog. | User sees personalized results before uploading a single item. Proves the app "gets" them immediately. Bridges the empty-wardrobe gap. |
| **Progressive wardrobe building** — gentle nudges, not requirements | After each outfit viewed: "Add the top you're wearing right now?" One-tap photo. Gradually builds wardrobe through daily micro-actions. | Distributes the catalog pain across weeks instead of concentrating it in one agonizing session. Wardrobe grows organically alongside usage. |

### RETAIN: Build the Daily Habit

| Feature | What It Does | Why It Drives Growth |
|---|---|---|
| **"Wearing this today" one-tap log** | Bottom of each outfit card: "Wear this" button. Logs the outfit + date + weather automatically. | Builds wear history data. Enables cost-per-wear, "you haven't worn X in 3 months," and smarter future suggestions. Also creates a sense of investment — the more you log, the more the app knows you. |
| **Outfit feedback loop** — save / skip / "not my style" | Swipe right = save. Swipe left = skip. | **"Rediscover" prompts** — surface items not worn in 30+ days | Once a week: "You haven't worn your olive chinos in 34 days. Here are 3 outfits with them." | Solves the "same 2-3 items" repetition problem that plagues Acloset/Cladwell. Makes users feel like their wardrobe is bigger than they thought. Sustainability hook. |

### REFER: Plant the Seeds

| Feature | What It Does | Why It Drives Growth |
|---|---|---|

### ACQUIRE + RETAIN: "Will It Work?" — Try Before You Buy (HEADLINE FEATURE)

| Feature | What It Does | Why It Drives Growth |
|---|---|---|
| **Share a link → instant wardrobe analysis** | User browsing Zara/Uniqlo shares product link to MIEN → app scrapes item data (JSON-LD / OG tags) → creates virtual item → runs fit engine → shows verdict: "WORTH IT: creates 8 new outfits" or "THINK TWICE: you own 2 similar jackets" | **3-5x usage frequency increase.** Users open the app every time they shop online (3-5x/week) — not just when getting dressed. Transforms the app from "morning helper" to "always-on fashion advisor." |
| **Snap a photo in-store → same analysis** | User in H&M takes photo of jacket on rack → Claude Vision classifies it (category, color, material) → same fit engine pipeline → verdict screen | **Captures offline shopping moments.** The app becomes indispensable for in-store decisions. No competitor exists in this moment. |
| **Clipboard detection** | User copies product URL → opens MIEN → "Analyze this item?" prompt appears | **Zero-friction entry.** Removes even the "share" step. Fastest path from "I want this" to "should I buy this?" |
| **Verdict screen with outfit previews** | Shows: body fit score, style match, color harmony, new outfit count, duplicate detection ("you own 2 similar items"), est. cost-per-wear, and swipeable outfit carousel with the new item | **Builds trust through honesty.** Sometimes the answer is "SKIP IT." That honesty creates the viral story: "this app saved me from a bad purchase." No shopping app will ever tell you NOT to buy. |
| **Wishlist** | Items analyzed but not purchased → saved with verdict data. Re-analyze if wardrobe changes. Price drop notifications. | **Retention hook independent of outfit suggestions.** Users check wishlist for price drops. Also creates a purchase intent dataset for future monetization. |

**Technical approach (validated):**
- Link scraping: Supabase Edge Function → fetch HTML → parse JSON-LD (covers 80-90% of Uniqlo, Zara, H&M, ASOS, Nike, etc.) → fallback to OG tags → fallback to Claude Vision on product image
- Photo analysis: Claude Vision API via edge function → structured JSON classification
- Share sheet: `expo-share-intent` (drop-in Expo plugin, works with Expo Router)
- Virtual item injection: extend existing `generateOutfits()` pipeline — inject virtual ClothingItem, filter results to outfits containing it

**Why this is the moat:** Requires body measurements + style profile + full wardrobe + working fit engine. Competitors would need to rebuild from scratch. No shortcut.

See full spec: `docs/feature-try-before-you-buy.md`

## The Growth Flywheel (How It All Connects)

```
USER ADDS ITEMS ──→ BETTER OUTFIT SUGGESTIONS ──→ USER WEARS & LOGS
       ↑                                                    │
       │                                                    ▼
  WARDROBE GROWS ←── MOTIVATION TO ADD MORE ←── "THIS ACTUALLY WORKS"
       │                                                    │
       ▼                                                    ▼
  MORE DATA FOR AI ──→ SMARTER SUGGESTIONS ──→ DAILY HABIT FORMS
       │                                                    │
       │                                                    ▼
       │                                            USER SHARES OUTFIT
       │                                                    │
       │                                                    ▼
       │                                            FRIEND DOWNLOADS
       │                                                    │
       │                                                    ▼
       │                                            NEW USER ADDS ITEMS ──→ ...
       │
       ▼
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  "WILL IT WORK?" SHOPPING LOOP (Stage 2+)  │
  │                                             │
  │  USER SEES ITEM ONLINE/IN-STORE             │
  │       │                                     │
  │       ▼                                     │
  │  SHARES LINK / TAKES PHOTO                  │
  │       │                                     │
  │       ▼                                     │
  │  APP SHOWS VERDICT + OUTFIT PREVIEWS        │
  │       │                                     │
  │       ├──→ "WORTH IT" → USER BUYS           │
  │       │        │                             │
  │       │        ▼                             │
  │       │    ADDS TO WARDROBE                  │
  │       │    (wardrobe grows, loop continues)  │
  │       │                                      │
  │       ├──→ "SKIP IT" → USER SAVES MONEY     │
  │       │        │                             │
  │       │        ▼                             │
  │       │    SHARES "APP SAVED ME $89"         │
  │       │    (viral referral moment)           │

  │                │                             │

  └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**The Shopping Loop is the second flywheel.** It runs 3-5x more frequently than the outfit loop (users shop more often than they plan outfits) and creates the strongest viral moments (purchase decision stories).

### The Flywheel Accelerators (Features That Spin It Faster)

| Accelerator | How It Works |
|---|---|
| **Quick Start (3-5 items)** | Gets the outfit flywheel spinning in 5 minutes instead of 8 hours |
| **AI auto-tagging** | Removes friction from "User Adds Items" step |
| **Visible AI learning** | Strengthens "This Actually Works" conviction |
| **Shareable outfit cards** | Amplifies "User Shares Outfit" step |
| **"Will It Work?" share sheet** | Creates the shopping flywheel — 3-5x more app opens per day |
| **Verdict screenshots** | "This app told me not to buy this" — strongest viral story in the category |
| **Style DNA / Wrapped** | Creates viral moments that bring waves of new users |
| **Cost-per-wear** | Adds a retention hook independent of outfit suggestions |
| **Progressive wardrobe building** | Keeps "Wardrobe Grows" step continuous, not one-time |
| **Wishlist + price drops** | Re-engagement hook tied to real purchase intent |

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do (Learned from Competitor Deaths)

| Mistake | Who Did It | What Happened | Our Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paywall after time investment** | Acloset (100-item cap) | Mass 1-star reviews, users feel betrayed | NEVER cap items on free tier |
| **Ads between actions** | Acloset, Pureple | *"Unusable"*, users flee to Whering | NEVER show ads. Ever. |
| **Remove beloved features** | Cladwell (May 2021 rebrand) | 200-day streak users left permanently | ADD features, never remove |
| **Ignore user feedback** | Smart Closet (dev never responds) | Users feel abandoned, stop trusting app | Respond to every review. Ship visible improvements from feedback. |
| **Subscription traps** | Cladwell ($101.99 surprise charge) | Trust destroyed, legal complaints | Transparent pricing. Easy cancellation. Confirm before any charge. |
| **Overpromise AI** | Acloset ("AI outfit planner") | *"The AI just isn't there yet"* → disappointment | Under-promise, over-deliver. Call it "smart suggestions." Let users discover the intelligence. |
| **Social features nobody asked for** | Whering (friend wardrobes) | *"Kind of pointless"* without network | Build social as OPT-IN layer, never core requirement |
| **Platform exclusivity** | Stylebook (iOS only since 2010) | 50% of market permanently excluded | iOS + Android from Day 1 (React Native gives us this) |

## The One Page Summary

```
STAGE 0 (Week 1-4):     Build the magic moment
                         "Add 3 items → see your first outfit in 5 minutes"

STAGE 1 (Week 5-10):    Prove it works with real users
                         Bulk upload + feedback loop + shareable outfit cards
                         50-100 beta users, 40% Day 7 retention

STAGE 2 (Week 11-18):   Launch with "Will It Work?"
                         App Store + "Will It Work?" (share link → verdict)
                         + cost-per-wear + calendar + TikTok content
                         1,000+ downloads/week, 4.5+ rating

STAGE 3 (Month 5-9):    Grow and go viral
                         Style DNA + AI try-on + closet twins + travel mode
                         + proactive shopping recommendations
                         50K+ MAU, 15% referral rate

STAGE 4 (Month 10-15):  Monetize without betraying
                         Premium ($5.99/mo) + affiliate shopping + Style Wrapped
                         5% conversion, $500K+ ARR

STAGE 5 (Month 16+):    Own the category
                         AI chat stylist + Apple Watch + "MIEN generation"
                         Cultural brand, not just an app
```

The app serves TWO daily moments:
1. **Morning**: "What should I wear today?" → outfit feed
2. **Shopping**: "Should I buy this?" → "Will It Work?" verdict

Every feature serves one purpose: **make the user dress better, buy smarter, and tell a friend.**
