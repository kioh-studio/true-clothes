# Verification: Google Photos Wardrobe status + competitor pricing/ratings (as of 2026-10-10)

Method note: ~19 calls. WebFetch summaries (small-model extraction) of listings; Google Play pages returned 404/empty so NO Google Play ratings/install brackets were verified (see Gaps). Google's own blog/support pages were not re-fetched; rollout facts come from 9to5Google (citing Google's support-forum post) and press.

## Google Photos Wardrobe: shipped? where? what?

### Takeaway
Yes, it shipped: Android from early June 2026, iOS from ~24 Sept 2026, but only in the US, India and Brazil. Vietnam and all of Southeast Asia are NOT included. Initial access required a paid Google AI Pro/Ultra plan; current gating is unclear.

### Cited Findings
- Rollout started "slowly" ~2026-06-08 on Android, in US, Brazil, India, "more regions to follow"; iPhone/iPad "in the future". Google confirmed in a Photos support-forum post. — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/06/08/google-photos-wardobe-rolling-out/)
- Requirements at launch: Google AI Pro or AI Ultra subscribers "plus other select users" ("at least for now"), minimum age for the country, Face Groups on, user selects which face is theirs. — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/06/08/google-photos-wardobe-rolling-out/)
- Function: scans photo library, identifies clothing, sorts into closet, mix-and-match outfits, save outfits, virtual try-on (preview on a digital avatar of self). Originally announced 2026-04-29. — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/06/08/google-photos-wardobe-rolling-out/); announcement: [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/photos/google-photos-wardrobe-feature/) (cited in platform_threat.md, not re-fetched)
- iOS: Wardrobe now on Android and iOS, only US, India, Brazil; "no Canadian availability announced". That article does not mention a subscription requirement for Wardrobe. — [iPhoneinCanada 2026-09-25](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/09/25/google-photos-adds-5-new-features-but-canada-misses-some/); search summary attributing iOS arrival to ~Sept 24 via MacRumors/Gadget Hacks: [Gadget Hacks](https://android.gadgethacks.com/news/google-photos-wardrobe-update-tests-add-regenerate-tools/) (search snippet only)
- Reported test of "regenerate" tools in Wardrobe (snippet only, unverified detail) — [Gadget Hacks](https://android.gadgethacks.com/news/google-photos-wardrobe-update-tests-add-regenerate-tools/)

### Inferences
- With no SEA/Vietnam launch, Wardrobe is not currently a direct competitor in MIEN's target market; the risk is a future country expansion.
- Paid-tier gating at launch limits the "free default app" threat described in platform_threat.md, though iOS coverage suggests gating may have loosened (unconfirmed).

### Gaps
- No usage numbers or hands-on reviews found. Shopping links not mentioned in any source seen. Whether the AI Pro/Ultra requirement still applies after the iOS launch: unconfirmed (sources conflict by omission). Google's support page itself not fetched.

## Competitor pricing and ratings (US App Store listings fetched 2026-10-10)

### Takeaway
All seven are cheap to mid-priced; only Style DNA lists personal color analysis plus figure-type guidance plus an "item match check" (closest to a buy verdict). Acloset has personal color and "fit diagnosis". None lists body-measurement-based garment fit scoring.

### Cited Findings
- Alta Daily (Flagship AI): App Store 4.9 from 16K ratings; no IAP listed, free ("all features free right now" per a reviewer). Features: weather/schedule outfits, receipts/photo import, cost-per-wear, packing, closet-gap shopping recs, avatar try-on. No measurements, color analysis or buy verdict listed. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6481705400). Play: ~219.9K installs is a third-party estimate; $11M seed April 2026 per Preqin snippet — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.alta)
- Indyx: 4.8 from 1.6K; IAP Monthly $12.99, Annual $74.99. Free site claims unlimited items/outfits free. No measurements/fit/color analysis/buy verdict. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1599179405); [myindyx.com](https://www.myindyx.com)
- Whering: 4.7 from 12K (US); IAP: credits 10/$2.99, 50/$7.99, 100/$12.99; Outfit Maker $4.99; "Supporter" tiers $0.99-$49.99. No monthly/annual subscription listed. Colour palette feature (go-to hues and gaps), cost-per-wear; no measurements/fit/verdict. UK listing 4.6 from 2.5K; store text claims 9M+ users (self-reported). — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1519461680); [UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/whering-your-digital-wardrobe/id1519461680)
- Acloset (Looko): 4.4 from 5.4K; free to 100 items. IAP: Basic $3.99/mo, $27.99/yr; Premium $9.99/mo, $59.99/yr; Expert $24.99/mo, $147.99/yr; "beans" $1.99-$9.99. Personal color analysis: yes; "fit diagnosis" of flattering silhouettes: yes; measurements: no; release notes mention "Style Evaluation" (undescribed). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/acloset-ai-fashion-assistant/id1542311809)
- Stylebook: paid app $4.99 (no subscription/IAP listed), 4.7 from 8.8K. No measurements/fit/color analysis/verdict (has "try items with your clothes before buying" in Shop). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stylebook/id335709058)
- Style DNA: 4.3 from 7.5K. IAP: monthly $7.99/$9.99/$19.99; 3-month $14.99/$19.99; annual $19.99/$29.99/$39.99; Palette and Body Type Guide $12.99; Advanced Color Palette $9.99 (multiple price points, tier mapping not stated). Features: 12-season color analysis from selfie, body/figure-type guide, "Item match check" (photograph store item, see if it suits style profile and figure type), measurements only "suggested" for better outfits. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1358319821)
- GetWardrobe: App Store 4.3 from 786; free 100 items/outfits; site: Premium $9.99/mo, $49.99/yr with annual trial — [getwardrobe.com/pricing](https://getwardrobe.com/pricing/); App Store IAP list shows Month $4.99 and $9.99, Year $34.99 and $49.99 (two sets, unexplained), AI credits $4.99-$59.99, stylist access $69.99/mo or $690/yr. "Sizes Notebook" records brand fit; no color analysis, measurements, or verdict. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id656212466)

### Inferences
- Closest overlap with MIEN's differentiators: Style DNA (color + figure type + item check) and Acloset (color + silhouette fit). Neither uses numeric body measurements or garment-measurement fit scoring per their listings.
- Premium price band for annual plans: ~$28-$75 (Acloset Basic $27.99 to Indyx $74.99); Alta is free.

### Gaps
- Google Play rating and install bracket for all seven: not obtained (Play pages 404/empty via fetch). Alta's Android installs only via third-party estimate.
- Whering has no verified monthly/annual subscription (listing shows credits/one-time items); its own pricing page not fetched.
- Alta monetisation: listing shows no IAP; unknown whether paywall exists in-app or planned.
- Ratings are US storefront on 2026-10-10 from tool-extracted text; counts vary by region.
- Not checked: Google Wardrobe usage numbers, regional pricing in Vietnam.
