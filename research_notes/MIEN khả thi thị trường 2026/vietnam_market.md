# Vietnam (and SEA) market for a consumer AI stylist / digital wardrobe / personal color app (as of 2026-10)

Coverage note: ~19 tool calls, WebSearch snippets plus 4 fetched pages. Several key questions had NO retrievable primary data (flagged under Gaps). FX is approximate (~25,000-26,000 VND per USD).

## 1. Local competitors and adjacent products

### Takeaway
I found no evidence of a Vietnamese-built AI wardrobe/stylist app in search results; the visible competition is foreign apps (some with Vietnamese UI) and Google's built-in wardrobe feature. Nothing found on Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop VN try-on, or on Coolmate/Canifa/YODY/Routine styling features (absence of search hits is not proof of absence).

### Cited Findings
- Google Photos is rolling out an AI "tủ đồ" that auto-builds a digital wardrobe from personal photos and suggests outfits; Android first, then iOS; status in Vietnam unverified — [Sforum/CellphoneS](https://cellphones.com.vn/sforum/cach-tao-tu-quan-ao-ai-tren-google-photos), [Quantrimang](https://quantrimang.com/tao-va-phoi-do-tu-anh-ca-nhan-voi-google-photos-214890)
- "AI Stylist" (mwm.ai listing) has Vietnamese UI, smart wardrobe + occasion outfits; developer's country not stated — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/ai-stylist/6741504251)
- Dress AI (digital wardrobe, background removal, credit system; Plus 60 / Pro 200 credits per month) and DressUp (outfits by weather/mood) are international apps; Vietnamese support unknown — [Peerlist Dress AI](https://peerlist.io/yongho/project/dress-ai), [DressUp App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6746646997)
- Other international wardrobe tools surfaced: Fits, Wardrobe, Qlode; Goodsize is a B2B size-recommendation widget for brand websites — [Fits](https://www.fits-app.com/knowledge/find-gaps-in-your-wardrobe), [Goodsize](https://goodsize.helpscoutdocs.com/article/54-does-this-really-work)
- Search for Shopee/VN-marketplace virtual try-on returned only Shopify third-party apps (VibeTry, Sizebay), nothing on Shopee itself — [VibeTry](https://apps.shopify.com/vibetry?locale=th)

### Inferences
- Whitespace for a Vietnamese-language, VN-sizing wardrobe app appears real, but low search visibility of competitors could also mean a small/unattractive category. Google Photos wardrobe is a free-substitute risk for basic cataloguing.

### Gaps
- No data on Coolmate/Canifa/YODY/Routine/Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop VN styling or fit features; no Korean/Chinese stylist app penetration data in VN; no store-rank or download data for any wardrobe app in VN. Needs direct App Store/Play VN checks.

## 2. Personal color trend in Vietnam

### Takeaway
Personal color is an established paid in-person service in HCMC at roughly 0.7-5M VND per session, driven by Korean TikTok content; free/freemium AI selfie apps already exist, which caps app pricing power.

### Cited Findings
- Tuoi Tre (Sept 2024): personal color consulting in HCMC costs VND 690,000-5,000,000 (US$29-210 per the article) per session, up to 2 hours; studios in District 7 and Tan Binh named (incl. Thao Jaly's); described as "relatively recent" in Vietnam; no customer counts — [Tuoi Tre News](https://news.tuoitre.vn/is-color-analysis-beauty-without-surgery-in-ho-chi-minh-city-10381748.htm)
- Vietcetera: basic session ~800,000-2,000,000+ VND; "a few" dedicated facilities; TikTok topic "Korean personal color analysis" >300M views; phone apps and TikTok effects used for at-home checks (year not shown on page, likely 2023-24) — [Vietcetera](https://vietcetera.com/vn/personal-color-ban-co-mau-gi)
- SGGP: ~1M to >5M VND for 1-3 sessions, online or in person — [SGGP](https://www.sggp.org.vn/di-tim-mau-sac-ca-nhan-post756011.html) (search summary only; page not opened)
- Existing AI personal-color apps on iOS (several with Vietnamese listing, mostly free + IAP/paywalled results): StyleIt AI, Color Analysis AI: Style Match, Season Palette, Blossom (12-season), Toned AI, YouCam Makeup — [StyleIt AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%EA%B0%9C%EC%9D%B8-%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80%EC%9D%BC%EB%A6%AC%EC%8A%A4%ED%8A%B8-%EC%BB%AC%EB%9F%AC-%ED%8C%94%EB%A0%88%ED%8A%B8-styleit-ai/id6670424652?l=vi), [Color Analysis AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%EB%84%90%EC%BB%AC%EB%9F%AC%EC%A7%84%EB%8B%A8-%EC%9D%B8%EA%B3%B5%EC%A7%80%EB%8A%A5-%EC%BB%AC%EB%9F%AC/id6747317168?l=vi), [Season Palette](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/season-palette-color-analysis/id6740197585), [Blossom](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757105218)

### Inferences
- Human-session anchor of ~1-2M VND (~$40-80) makes a low-priced AI result look cheap, but the crowded app category means the differentiator must be wardrobe-linked utility, not the analysis itself.

### Gaps
- No 2025-26 search-volume/Google Trends data; no count of studios; no paid-conversion data for any personal-color app in VN; TikTok creator pricing not found.

## 3. Willingness to pay

### Takeaway
Reliable Vietnam subscription benchmarks (ARPU, paid conversion, price points, iOS/Android split) were NOT found. Available data is game-dominated and implies a low-spend, ad-monetised market.

### Cited Findings
- Adjust, Vietnam 2026 report (published 2026-05-27; data to Mar 2026): 2.88B downloads in 2025 (11th globally); gaming IAP revenue US$259M; video streaming IAP US$23.2M (+73%); finance downloads 172M; no subscription, iOS/Android, or CPI figures for Vietnam in the summary — [ppc.land summary](https://ppc.land/vietnams-app-market-hits-2-88b-downloads-what-the-data-really-shows/)
- AppMagic (via Google Apps Summit 2025): Vietnam app+game IAP revenue grew 65%, fastest in APAC; Google Play paid >VND 2T (US$75.9M) from OVERSEAS consumers to Vietnamese developers (not domestic spend) — [Viet Nam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1729500/google-apps-summit-2025-spotlights-viet-nam-as-apac-s-fastest-growth-engine.html)
- MoMo has ~68-70%+ e-wallet share and supports recurring payments; became a YouTube Premium payment channel Apr 2023; listed as a payment method outside App Store/Play billing — [Coda](https://www.coda.co/payment-guides/momo/), [MoMo](https://www.momo.vn/tin-tuc/tin-tuc-su-kien/momo-la-1-trong-10-ung-dung-duoc-nguoi-viet-su-4124)
- One Vietnamese AppMagic write-up claims iOS IAP +5.46% and Android slightly down in 2025, scope (global vs VN) unclear — [Substack summary](https://thestackbytracy.substack.com/p/appmagic-monetization-report-2025) (unverified)

### Inferences
- Revenue per Vietnamese user is probably far below US/JP/KR, and iOS is likely the paying segment (urban affluent), consistent with App Store-first strategy. Estimate, not data.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower/data.ai/Appota/Decision Lab/Q&Me subscription ARPU or conversion; no VN iOS vs Play split; no VND price-tier norms; no in-app payment friction data.

## 4. Fashion e-commerce and consumption

### Takeaway
Online fashion is large and growing, but sources differ ~5x on size by scope; TikTok Shop is the growth channel. No return-rate/fit-problem data was found.

### Cited Findings
- Metric.vn via Viet Nam News (Jan 2026): Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada, Tiki together ~VND 429 trillion in 2025 (~US$16-17B), +34.8% y/y; women's fashion third category at ~VND 54.5T (~US$2.1B) — [Viet Nam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1763770/vietnamese-spend-45-million-a-day-online-shopping.html)
- Metric via Vietnam Briefing (Chinese): Shopee ~56% share (Q3 2025 revenue +4%), TikTok Shop revenue +69% to ~41% — [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnams-e-commerce-sector-outlook-in-2026-cn.html/)
- ECDB (modelled estimate): Vietnam fashion e-commerce US$4.74B in 2025 — [ECDB](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/market/vn/fashion); Ken Research says "fashion & lifestyle platforms" US$27B — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/vietnam-e-commerce-fashion-lifestyle-platforms-market.md); conflicting scope, Ken is low-reliability
- Second-hand: Gen Z intent driven by attitudes and social norms (2026 review); TikTok short video raises intent (n=363 Hanoi Gen Z, 2025); Brands Vietnam's 5.6B reusable items figure rests on 2020 data (old) — [Tap chi Kinh te Tai chinh](https://nghiencuu.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/tong-quan-ly-thuyet-ve-hanh-vi-tieu-dung-thoi-trang-secondhand-cua-gen-z-tai-viet-nam-tiep-can-tich-hop-tpb-va-sdt-163004.html), [VJOL](https://vjol.info.vn/vwa/article/view/115843), [Brands Vietnam](https://www.brandsvietnam.com/23300-Lam-moi-thoi-trang-secondhand)

### Inferences
- A pre-purchase verdict fits a high-volume, livestream-driven impulse environment, but user value is unproven here.

### Gaps

## 6. Regulatory and operational

### Takeaway
PDPL (Law 91/2025/QH15, effective 2026-01-01) plus Decree 356/2025/ND-CP (effective 2026-01-01) covers biometric data (Art. 31); body/selfie handling needs careful consent. Apple changed VN tax handling Aug 2025.

### Cited Findings
- PDPL passed 2025-06-26, effective 2026-01-01; Art. 31 covers location and biometric data; Decree 356/2025/ND-CP issued 2025-12-31; max fine for cross-border transfer violations 5% of prior-year revenue — [thuvienphapluat Decree 356](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Quyen-dan-su/Nghi-dinh-356-2025-ND-CP-huong-dan-Luat-Bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-687428.aspx) (page 403 on fetch; from search snippets), [hethongphapluat](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-2025.html/dieu-1)
- Apple tax update effective 2025-08-21: Vietnam-based individual developers: 2% PIT replaces CIT, plus 5% FCT on Apple's commission; Vietnam-based organisations: Apple stops remitting FCT on end-customer sales, 5% FCT on commission; foreign orgs VAT 5% to 10% — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/apple-announces-tax-and-pricing-updates-affecting-global-developers/), [Apple Developer News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=qs5bol0). Verify with a VN tax adviser.

### Inferences
- On-device body/selfie processing reduces PDPL exposure (no storage or transfer); cloud try-on/face-composite sends sensitive data out and would trigger consent and possibly cross-border rules.

### Gaps
- Exact Decree 356 text on sensitive-data consent, impact assessment filing, startup/SME grace periods, minors; Google Play VN tax treatment; app distribution requirements.
